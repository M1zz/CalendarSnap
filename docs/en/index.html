Questions

What kinds of photos work?

Monthly calendars (grids), newsletters written as sentences, and single notices or flyers. Shooting straight on so the text is sharp helps a lot, and you can pick several photos to scan at once.

Some events are missing or a title has a typo.

Handwriting and faint print can be hard to read. Fix the title, date or what to bring right in the list, or swipe away anything you don't need. Add a missing event with Add Event Manually on the Scan tab, then save again.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed in Settings > Notifications > Kids Schedule. In the app's settings (⚙️) you can also choose which reminders you get.

Events for other classes show up too.

Enter your child's class name in Settings, and events meant only for other classes are filtered out. When your child moves up a year, just change it there.

I have more than one child.

Add as many children as you like in Settings, each with a profile photo. Choose whose events you're scanning, and the calendar shows each child in their own color, with a combined view and a filter per child.

How do I share the schedule with my partner?

In Settings, under Schedule Sharing, tap Invite People and send the link by message. They can see and edit every child and event, and changes from either side show up on both right away. This is separate from Apple Family Sharing.

Are there other ways to share?

The share button on the Calendar tab sends a Kids Schedule file, a calendar file (.ics) or a text summary. With Also add to Apple Calendar on, a calendar named after your child is created, and sharing it over iCloud in the Calendar app keeps your family in sync too.

Are my photos or events sent anywhere?

No. Text in your photos is read entirely on your device, and data is stored only on your device. Events go through your iCloud only when you invite someone with Schedule Sharing, and only to the people you invited.
