Privacy Policy

Effective: August 20, 2026 · Last updated: October 16, 2026

Kids Schedule (the "App") takes your privacy seriously. The App does not collect or send personal information such as photos, events or details about your children to the developer. By default, this data is processed and stored only on your device. The only things the developer receives are anonymous usage statistics and crash reports that can't identify you (see Section 4).

The one exception: if you turn on Family Sharing and invite family members yourself, your schedule data is stored in your own iCloud account and shared with the family members you invited. See Section 3 for details. Family Sharing is optional — if you leave it off, your photos, events and children's details never leave your device.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal information. Everything below is stored only on your device. (Anonymous usage statistics that can't identify you are covered separately in Section 4.)

  • Photos: Calendar and notice photos are analyzed only by Apple's on-device Vision framework and are never sent anywhere. The original photos used for analysis are not saved in the App.
  • Schedule data: Extracted events (title, date, notes) are stored only in the App's private storage on your device (App Group).
  • Child profiles: Your children's names and profile photos are stored only on your device. (Profile photos are not shared even when Family Sharing is on.)

2. How We Use Device Permissions

  • Camera: To photograph calendars and notices (analyzed on your device right away)
  • Photo Library: To extract events from photos you choose
  • Calendars: To add extracted events to your Apple Calendar and check for duplicates (optional; can be turned off in Settings)
  • Notifications: Local reminders the day before and the day of an event (sent directly by your device; no push server)

3. Family Sharing (Optional, iCloud)

When you invite your partner or other family members under Settings > Family Sharing, you and the people you invite can view and edit the same schedule together. This feature uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) sharing. The developer cannot see or keep anything that is shared.

  • What is shared: Each event's title, date, time, all-day setting, what-to-bring notes and child's name, plus the list of children.
  • What is not shared: Calendar and notice photos, children's profile photos, and reminder settings.
  • Where it's stored: In the private iCloud database of the person who sent the invite (the owner). Apple's Privacy Policy also applies.
  • Who can access it: Only people the owner personally invited with an invite link and who accepted. Links are never made public.
  • Turning it off: At any time, go to Settings > Family Sharing and tap "Stop Sharing" (owner) or "Leave Sharing" (participant). After that, nothing syncs anymore, and any events already received stay on each person's device.

4. Anonymous Usage Statistics

To understand how many people use the App and to fix bugs, the App sends the following anonymous statistics to Apple iCloud (CloudKit). Only the developer can read them.

  • A random ID created at install (not linked to your device, Apple ID or any other app; it changes if you reinstall)
  • App version, iOS version, and region and language settings
  • How many times and on which days the App was opened, the install date, and days since install
  • Diagnostic reports when the App freezes or quits unexpectedly (App version, iOS version, device type, and technical details of the error)

This never includes your name, contact details, location, or anything you enter such as photos, events or your children's details. Statistics are sent only while you're signed in to iCloud.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

The App never sells personal information or provides it to third parties for advertising or analytics. It uses no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics services and no developer-run servers. Apple's iCloud infrastructure is used only when you turn on Family Sharing and to send the anonymous statistics described in Section 4.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your photos, events and children's details live on your device, deleting the App deletes all of the data it stored. If you use Family Sharing and the owner taps "Stop Sharing", invited family members no longer receive new events. Shared data stored in iCloud is cleaned up when the owner stops sharing or deletes the App. Events added to Apple Calendar can be managed and deleted directly in the iOS Calendar app.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a tool for parents and caregivers, and it does not collect children's personal information. The children's names and photos you enter are never sent to the developer. With Family Sharing on, your children's names and events go only to the family members you invited yourself.
